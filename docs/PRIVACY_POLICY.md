# Privacy policy and terms

The published versions are the only versions:

- **Privacy policy:** https://ample-money.vercel.app/privacy (source: `web/src/app/privacy/page.tsx`)
- **Terms of use:** https://ample-money.vercel.app/terms (source: `web/src/app/terms/page.tsx`)

The app links to both from Settings → About and from the account sign-in card (`lib/core/app_identity.dart`). Use the same two addresses in the store listings.

When something changes that the policy describes (what is stored, synced or sent, a new permission, a new service provider), update the page and its "Last updated" date in the same change.
